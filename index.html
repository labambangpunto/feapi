Getting Started

Follow these simple steps to install and start using Feapi.

1

Download the APK

Head to the download section above or our GitHub Releases page. Choose the APK package for Android.

2

Allow Unknown Sources

Open the downloaded APK file. When prompted, allow your browser or file manager to "Install apps from unknown sources" in your Android settings.

3

Initial Setup

Open the app. You'll be greeted with a quick setup wizard to define your primary currency and initial balance. No account creation needed!

4

Track & Analyze

Start adding your daily expenses and incomes. Visit the dashboard to see your spending trends and set up budgets for better financial health.

1

Download the Tarball

Head to the download section above or our GitHub Releases page. Choose the `.tar.gz` binary package for Linux.

2

Extract & Execute

Open your terminal and extract the archive:
tar -xzf Feapi-linux.tar.gz
Then, run the executable inside the extracted folder.

3

Initial Setup

Once launched, the app will guide you through a quick setup to define your primary currency and initial balance.

4

Track & Analyze

Start adding your expenses. Your financial data is securely stored locally on your Linux machine for complete privacy.
